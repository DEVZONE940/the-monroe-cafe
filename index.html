Good to know

How the order lands

  1. Your message reaches the manager's WhatsApp with the items, your name and phone.
  2. They reply with today's prices and how long it takes. Prices change, so this page never states them.
  3. For a group order, mention the number of people and they will prep accordingly.
Book a table

Pick a slot and it goes to the host desk.

Rooftop tables and cabanas fill up on weekends. Send the date and head count and the cafe confirms the table rather than leaving you waiting at the gate.

No booking engine sits behind this form, and it does not pretend to. It writes a proper booking message to the cafe's WhatsApp.
